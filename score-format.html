Score format

Scores are XML files based on the Step 5 format. The score fully defines the music: tempo, effects, and every note.

<score bpm="100" beatspermeasure="4">
  <effect slot="4" type="Reverb" decay="0.75"/>
  <instrument instrument="Subtractive">
    <note measure="1" beat="1" duration="1" note="C4" wave="sawtooth"
          amp="0.2" attack="0.01" release="0.1"
          cutoff="300" peak="3000" dry="0.7" send4="0.8"/>
  </instrument>
</score>
